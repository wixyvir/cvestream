# Decision Log

- 2026-10-26: Started a new product brief for cvestream. No existing project brief or project-context notes were found.
- 2026-10-26: User described the product idea: their workplace Slack channel displays the CERT-FR CVE alert feed (https://cert.cyber.gouv.fr/alerte/feed/); they want a CLI daemon to provide the same "CSS" while filtering alerts to a selected list of tools/products. Open questions: whether "CSS" means CVSS/severity or the alert presentation/content, intended daemon output/delivery, audience and stakes.
- 2026-10-26: User corrected "CSS" to RSS. Intended audience is their team. The tool should support multiple Slack channels and multiple upstream RSS feeds with different configurations. A product-to-channel mapping may use a key/value datastore or a CSV file; storage remains undecided. The project should expose a final RSS endpoint that can be added to a Slack channel so it receives only relevant security issues. This establishes the desired outcome and use context; deployment, exact filter matching, and source-feed scope remain open.
- 2026-10-26: User confirmed Slack need not receive or manage the product filter configuration; product-name filtering happens before Slack consumes the resulting RSS endpoint. Product-name matching is acceptable. Treating filtered RSS endpoints as the product interface for subscribers.
- 2026-10-26: Fast-path drafting selected by user. Draft frames cvestream as a CLI daemon consuming multiple RSS sources and serving per-configuration filtered RSS endpoints for Slack subscriptions. Product-name matching is the initial filter approach; Slack does not manage filter lists.
- 2026-10-26: Unresolved deployment, matching semantics, feed compatibility, operations, measurable success thresholds, and CSV-versus-key/value configuration storage are marked as assumptions or open questions in the brief/addendum rather than asserted as decisions.
- 2026-10-26: Following source research, user approved the direction to keep CERT-FR as a curated source, supplement it with structured CVE JSON/API data for product/version/CVSS attributes, enrich with CISA KEV exploitation status, and continue serving filtered RSS endpoints to Slack. NVD versus EUVD remains open for validation against the team's products and representative records. Source provenance and unknown/missing scores should be preserved.
- 2026-10-26: Finalization audit: the brief captures the product goal, team audience, per-endpoint product filters, structured-source direction, and Slack RSS delivery. CSV versus key/value storage, NVD versus EUVD, matching semantics, hosting, and operational thresholds remain open in the brief/addendum. The original "CSS" wording is retained here as conversation history and its correction to RSS is captured above; no meaningful decisions were set aside as process noise. Structural and prose editorial passes were applied to the brief first, then the addendum.
